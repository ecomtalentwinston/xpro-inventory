This folder is watched by the Inventory Forecast Simulator.

Place your Sellerboard Products Dashboard Excel exports here, named exactly:
  sellerboard_7day.xlsx
  sellerboard_14day.xlsx
  sellerboard_30day.xlsx
  sellerboard_60day.xlsx
  sellerboard_90day.xlsx

The downloader script (sellerboard_downloader.py) saves files here automatically.
Once files are present, open the app and click "Auto-load all from folder" in the
Business Reports tab — no manual drag-and-drop needed.
